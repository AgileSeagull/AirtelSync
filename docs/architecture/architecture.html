1Advisor

advisor/ — orchestrates the conversation
Language detect→LLM with tool calling→Output guard→Reply·Scripted fallback

The LLM talks; tools do the work: lookup_gstin · update_profile · recommend_bundle · check_product · handoff_to_human.

Guard: every ₹ amount and product ID must come from a tool result (one retry). A failed or slow turn (35 s) is answered by the rule-based EN / HI / Hinglish advisor.

tools: recommend_bundle · check_product

2Product engine

engine/ — deterministic, no AI
Eligibility rules→Pricing→3-tier recommender

Catalogue rules: city tier, staff, sites, active GSTIN, required companions. Price falls with term; 5 % off for 3+ categories; micro firms' setup waived on 12+ months; GST 18 %. Tiers carry reason codes.

3Smart KYC

kyc/ — automated, explainable
DPDP consent→Upload / DigiLocker→OCR (Tesseract)→Parse & repair IDs→6 check layers→Verdict→e-Sign

Checks: quality (blur, skew) · format (GSTIN / PAN / Udyam, checksum repair) · completeness · registry · cross-document consistency · recency. Verdict: approved · needs action (owner fixes) · review (staff queue) · blocked, each with reasons.

Privacy & security by design

  • DPDP consent ledger: purpose-bound, revocable
  • Chat never asks for Aadhaar, PAN or bank details; IDs typed in chat are masked
  • Encrypted vault and deletion of raw images after the decision
  • Audit log of consent, document, verification, review and assisted-mode events
  • Role-based console: partners see only leads routed to them
  • The AI cannot invent prices or products (output guard)

1Discover

  1. Owner chats or speaks in their language.
  2. Advisor sends the turn to Gemini, which calls tools: GSTIN lookup prefills the profile; the engine checks eligibility.
  3. Guard checks the draft reply; tier cards appear after ~5 questions.

2Quote & accept

  1. Owner picks a tier, toggles items, chooses the term.
  2. Checkout re-prices each change through the engine (rules, discounts, GST).
  3. Accept creates the order in state QuoteAccepted.

3Verify (Smart KYC)

  1. Owner gives consent, then uploads documents or uses DigiLocker.
  2. OCR and ID checks against the registries produce a verdict with reasons; unclear cases go to staff review.
  3. E-sign moves the order to KYCVerified.

4Activate & track

  1. UPI mandate, then installation is scheduled and completed.
  2. Reroutes and delays are handled in the state machine.
  3. Each step notifies the owner in-app and on WhatsApp, ending at Activated.

5Learn & route

  1. The console scores every lead and routes it: digital, partner or field.
  2. Stalled leads go on a re-engage list; staff clear the KYC review queue.
  3. Partners can help an owner in assisted mode, with the owner's code.
